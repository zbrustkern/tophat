## 📐 SVG Custom Iconography Specification

For UI icons, use vector SVG paths adhering to these geometric rules:
1. **Line Weight**: `strokeWidth={1.5}` or `2` for clean geometric symmetry.
2. **Stroke Colors**: `stroke="#D4AF37"` (Deco Gold) or `stroke="currentColor"`.
3. **Corner Join**: `strokeLinecap="square"` and `strokeLinejoin="miter"` to preserve sharp Art Deco angles (avoid rounded joints where possible).
4. **Glow Effects**: `drop-shadow-[0_0_8px_rgba(212,175,55,0.4)]` for active/selected state.
